About us

A family-owned American manufacturer.

For over 26 years, Innovative Disposables has been dedicated to manufacturing premium disposable underpads that restore comfort, protect dignity and deliver peace of mind to families and caregivers everywhere.

Our mission

We build trust with every layer.

Those who rely on our products deserve the best protection available. We value long-term relationships above all, knowing our success starts with yours.

We have helped lead the private label underpad industry through sustainable manufacturing practices and leak-proof technology, providing brands with high-quality, responsible underpad solutions.

Our goal has always been to deliver premium, customizable underpads that protect surfaces, safeguard health and elevate our partners’ brands.
