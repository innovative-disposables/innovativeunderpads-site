Where underpads are used

From hospital wards to the home.

  • Hospitals and acute care. Standard protocol for fluid containment, wound care and infection prevention.
  • Nursing homes and long-term care. Staff rely on underpads for nighttime routines and to protect bedding.
  • Home care. People with limited mobility or severe nighttime leakage use underpads to protect mattresses, wheelchairs and furniture.
  • Post-surgery recovery. A protective layer against wound drainage and post-surgical discharge.
