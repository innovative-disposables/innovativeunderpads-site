Manufacturing

Quality

Inspected at every stage.

Strict quality control runs from incoming material through to warehouse storage. We use only high-standard materials and do not allow second-class or unqualified material into production. Our quality assurance team inspects product throughout every run.

We are actively working toward ISO 9001 certification, and our quality management processes are already modeled on its guidelines.

The complex

Four buildings. One integrated operation.

Our South Plainfield complex brings manufacturing and warehousing together on a single site. Two buildings are dedicated to production and two to warehousing, so raw material arrives, becomes finished underpads and ships out without ever leaving our control.

  • Two manufacturing buildings housing our three high-speed production lines
  • Two large-scale warehouses for raw materials and finished goods
  • Deep raw material stock on site for uninterrupted production
  • Finished goods staged for prompt shipment, FOB our New Jersey plant
