How it works

From first call to first shipment.

  1. Specify

    Tell us your size, absorbency, backsheet color, count per bag and bag design, and your expected annual volume.

  2. Sample

    We send free samples. Request them by phone, by fax or through our quote form.

  3. Produce

    Once approved, your order runs on our lines, typically within 4 to 6 weeks.

  4. Ship

    We ship anywhere in the United States, FOB our South Plainfield plant.
